TextBlock Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

TextBlock is a default SMS/MMS application with local, on-device message filtering. This policy explains the information TextBlock accesses and how it is used.

Information TextBlock accesses

  • SMS and MMS contents, senders, recipients, timestamps, attachments, and conversation metadata, so TextBlock can send, receive, display, organize, quarantine, and block messages.
  • Contacts, so TextBlock can display names and photos, allow known contacts through filtering, and help you select recipients.
  • Phone and SIM information needed by Android to send and receive SMS/MMS messages.
  • Photos, videos, audio, and files only when you choose attachments, view received media, record audio, or import or export a backup.
  • Microphone input only when you initiate speech-to-text or audio recording.

Local processing

TextBlock's political-spam classification, correction history, quarantine records, preferences, and message database are processed and stored locally on your device. TextBlock does not operate a server that receives your conversations, contacts, filter decisions, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.

Sending and sharing

When you send an SMS or MMS, its contents and attachments are transmitted through your mobile carrier to the recipients you select. Carrier SMS/MMS is not end-to-end encrypted by TextBlock.

If you explicitly select an installed third-party blocking service, TextBlock may pass a phone number to that app to perform the requested check or block action. TextBlock does not sell personal information or use message data for advertising or marketing.

Retention and deletion

Messages and app settings remain on your device until you delete them, clear TextBlock's app data, or uninstall the app. TextBlock automatically deletes its own quarantine records and associated quarantined messages after 90 days. Backup files remain where you save them until you delete them.

Children

TextBlock is intended for adults and is not designed for children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when TextBlock's data practices change. The current version and its effective date will remain available on this page.
